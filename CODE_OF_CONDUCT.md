# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small hobby project; let's keep it a friendly place.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks, whatever the person's background, experience or opinions.
- Assume good intentions, give feedback on the work and not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Stay on topic and keep issues and pull requests constructive. No spam, advertising or begging.
- Never share other people's private information, and never post cookies, passwords or account details (yours either).
- This project is about managing **your own** accounts. Requests or contributions meant to harm other players, to abuse Roblox, to cheat, or to take over accounts
  are not welcome and will be closed.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem privately, use the
[private security reporting form](https://github.com/AO-Miko/roblox-manager/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.
